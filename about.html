About Wet2Dry Solutions

From a Gulf MEP technician to India's Water Tightening pioneer — the full story, team and training centre.

OUR FOUNDER

Titus Pereira

Born and brought up in South India where coconut farming and home water systems were a way of life, Titus grew up around plumbing. Engineers in his village worked at Travancore Titanium Products Ltd — he joined as a helper for 3 years, then spent 2 years at VSSC / ISRO (Thumba) and 2 years as a fabricator at English Indian Clays Ltd.

Curious and observant, he took professional plumbing education while completing high school, then spent 10 years in the Gulf in MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) across multiple roles.

Returning to India, he founded "Everyday, Every Maintenance" — electrical, plumbing, motor winding, fabrication, FRP & carpentry — which became Hi-Tech Professional Maintenance, Pimpri-Chinchwad (2009).

His quality-obsession moment

Supervising every site himself, he kept seeing the same trio — seepage, wall dampness, paint peel-out. Years of research followed. The result: Water Tightening as a permanent, root-cause system — and the company was renamed Wet2Dry Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (2012). First PCMC (Wakad, Hinjewadi, Baner, Wagholi), then Navi Mumbai, then Bengaluru with wife Mrs. Tinu Titus Pereira, and today 7 cities.

In-house inventions

Hot Air Drier (Quick Wall Dryer) + Impactor (removes accumulated floor water), paired with Dr. Fixit / Fosroc / Sika / MYK Schomburg chemicals.
